Frequently Asked B2B Questions

How quickly can physical sample boxes be delivered? ▼
Physical material sample boxes are dispatched via priority courier within 24 hours of request, arriving at architectural offices across Poland and Central Europe within 48 hours.
Do you provide certified BIM & Revit 3D objects? ▼
Yes, 100% of our facade panels, acoustic slats, and fire glass assemblies feature downloadable Revit (.RFA), ArchiCAD (.GSM), and AutoCAD (.DWG) BIM files with embedded material thermal and fire properties.
What fire safety certifications do BUILDEX materials hold? ▼
All materials supplied by BUILDEX Poland comply strictly with European Standard EN 13501-1, carrying ratings from Class A1 non-combustible for ceramic panels to EI-90 and EI-120 for fire-rated structural glass.
